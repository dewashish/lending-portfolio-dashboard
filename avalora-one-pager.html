i

Purpose & positioning.

Avalora replaces a stack of monthly Excel workbooks with a single dashboard that every risk role can use without losing the rigour of the originals.

Problem

Static workbooks, slow decisions

Portfolio quality reports were compiled monthly, emailed across teams, and reconciled by hand for each subsidiary. Risk decisions waited on someone finding the right tab.

Solution

Live, scope-aware, queryable

One dashboard for group, region, and subsidiary views. Same data structure as the existing PQRs. Excel export remains a one-click action for offline work.

Outcome

Decisions land sooner

Threshold breaches surface in real time. Cross-subsidiary roll-ups are computed correctly (AUM-weighted for rates, summed for monetary). Auditability is built in.

ii

How the system fits together.

Source systems feed Supabase via validated ingest endpoints. The query layer feeds the dashboard, the AI assistant, and the Excel exporter from the same tables.

v

Oversight & controls.

Avalora separates viewing from administration. Sensitive actions are scoped to roles, gated by PIN where appropriate, and logged for audit.

Identity
Role-based access
Four roles: super admin, CRO, product analyst, risk analyst. Profile and role live in Supabase; row-level security policies scope what each role can read. Custom JWT (jose, 7-day) issued after Supabase auth.
Authentication
Magic-link & password
CRO magic-link auto-login secured by HMAC token comparison (constant-time, Web Crypto). Service-role client used only to create the session; never exposed to the browser.
Threshold management
Risk Appetite Registry
Configurable tolerance and appetite limits per metric, resolvable by scope (group, region, subsidiary, business line, product). Edits made via a PIN-locked drawer. Twenty plus metrics per business line with RAG indicators on the dashboard.
Data quality
Ingest validation
Every upload through /api/ingest/* passes API-key auth, Zod schema validation, and FX conversion before upsert. Twenty endpoints cover consumer, corporate, trade, risk, and FX feeds. Each call is logged for replay.
Aggregation rigor
Weighted roll-ups
Rate metrics (ROI, FPD %, 30+ Amt %, NCL %) are AUM-weighted averaged across subsidiaries. Monetary metrics are summed via value_usd. The rule is encoded in the query layer so the dashboard, AI assistant, and Excel exporter all behave identically.
Monitoring
Sentry & data status
Sentry Next.js captures client and server errors. A data-status surface in the UI shows freshness per source, last successful ingest, and any validation failures.

vii

Who works in it, and where they start.

Four roles, four landing patterns. The dashboard is shared; the path through it differs.

Persona i
Chief Risk Officer

Opens the Group Overview to scan headline KPIs, the breach panel, and the subsidiary heatmap. Drills into Consumer for Stage 2+3 share and 30+ DPD trend. Exports the PQR for board packs.

Persona ii
Risk Analyst

Lives in Delinquency, Collections, and Vintage. Uses the heatmaps to find the bucket or cohort that's slipping, then pulls roll-rate and vintage sheets from the PQR for the underlying numbers.

Persona iii
Product Analyst

Starts on Products and Origination. Watches which SKU is converting, what the average ticket is, and which products carry the worst non-starter rate. Uses the AI panel for cross-product comparisons.

Persona iv
Credit Policy Owner

Reviews TDD, Risk Analytics, and the Approved & Rejected base. Confirms the accepted population matches the policy band and that rejection reasons are concentrated where expected.
