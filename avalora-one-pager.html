i

Purpose.

Risk teams should spend their time deciding, not assembling. Avalora is built so the answer to a portfolio question takes seconds, not days.

Problem

Static workbooks, slow decisions

Portfolio quality reports were compiled monthly, emailed around, and reconciled by hand for every subsidiary. Risk decisions waited on whoever knew which tab to open.

Approach

Live, scope-aware, queryable

One dashboard for every level of the organisation. The structure of the original reports is preserved. Excel export is still a one-click action for offline work.

Outcome

Faster, fairer, auditable

Threshold breaches surface in real time. Group roll-ups are computed correctly without manual reconciliation. Every number traces back to its source.

v

Oversight & controls.

Viewing is separated from administration. Sensitive actions are scoped to roles, gated where appropriate, and logged end to end.

Identity
Role-based access
Different roles see different surfaces. Read access is scoped by role; administrative actions are restricted to authorised users only.
Authentication
Secure session
Time-limited sessions with cryptographically signed tokens. Constant-time secret comparison on shared links to prevent leakage through timing.
Threshold management
Risk-appetite registry
Configurable tolerance and appetite limits per metric, resolvable by scope (group, region, subsidiary, business line, product). Edits gated behind a separate confirmation step.
Data quality
Validated ingest
Every incoming feed is schema-validated, currency-normalised, and audit-logged before it touches the data store. Bad payloads are rejected, not silently coerced.
Aggregation rigour
Correct roll-ups
Rate metrics are weighted by exposure across subsidiaries; monetary metrics are summed in a common currency. The rule is encoded once so every surface behaves identically.
Monitoring
Health & freshness
Error capture for client and server. A data-status surface shows freshness per source, the last successful ingest, and any validation failures still outstanding.

Who works in it.

The dashboard is shared across the risk function. The path through it differs by role.

α
Strategic
Chief Risk Officer

Opens the consolidated view to scan headline metrics, breach signals, and the subsidiary heatmap. Drills into delinquency trends when a number deserves a second look.

β
Investigative
Risk Analyst

Lives in delinquency, collections, and vintage. Uses the heatmaps to find the bucket or cohort that's slipping, then pulls the underlying detail for the write-up.

γ
Operational
Product Analyst

Starts on products and origination. Watches which lines are converting, what the average ticket size is, and which products carry the worst early-default rate.

δ
Governance
Credit Policy Owner

Reviews bureau analytics, approved and rejected populations, and rejection reasons. Confirms the accepted book matches the policy band and tolerance.
