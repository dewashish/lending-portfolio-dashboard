What a bad signal looks like

Roll Forward Spike

A row in the roll-rate heatmap shifts from green to red — accounts are moving from 1–30 DPD to 31–60 DPD faster than last month.

Cure Rate Drop

Roll-backward % falls below tolerance — collections is failing to bring delinquent accounts back to current.

Vintage Decay

A recent cohort hits 5% delinquency at MOB-3 while older cohorts only crossed that line at MOB-6 — underwriting drift.

The Consumer PQR.

A one-click Excel artefact that mirrors the dashboard and lets risk, finance, and audit work with the underlying numbers offline.

What it is

Fourteen-sheet workbook

Generated client-side via ExcelJS from the same query layer the dashboard uses. Every sheet is a flat, pivot-ready dataset — no merged cells, formatted percentages, currencies, and dates.

ExcelJS .xlsx tab-coloured
Why it exists

Offline truth, identical to the screen

Boards want PDFs. Analysts want Excel. PQR closes that gap — same scope filter, same data, fully transparent. Auditors can trace every dashboard number to a sheet.

Sheets 1 – 7 · Portfolio performance.

The first half of the workbook covers the live portfolio — what's on book, where it sits in DPD, and how it's flowing between buckets.

SHEET 01
Portfolio Summary

Pivoted time-series of overall consumer KPIs (AUM, 30+ DPD, NPL, Stage mix) by period.

SHEET 02
Product Metrics

Long-form per-product / per-metric rows pivoted across periods. AUM, disbursement, delinquency per SKU.

SHEET 03
Net Flow Rates

Monthly net flow across DPD buckets — how the portfolio composition is changing period over period.

SHEET 04
Roll Rates

Bucket-to-bucket transition probabilities, period-pivoted. Forward and backward flows.

SHEET 05
Collections

Per-portfolio / per-bucket: amount, transitions, normalized %, roll-backward, stabilized, roll-forward.

SHEET 06
Vintage Analysis

Cohort × MOB rows with loan amount, delinquency rate, metric type. Drives the vintage heatmap.

SHEET 07
Non-Starters

Category × Product × Metric, pivoted across months. First-payment-default analysis.

Pivot convention

Time-series sheets share a single utility — pivotTimeSeries() — that turns long records into a metric × period grid with consistent header formatting. The result is identical handling across PQRs, so a user who knows the Consumer report can navigate Group, Trade, Corporate, or Risk PQRs without re-learning the structure.

How it's built
11 / 14

From ingest to Excel.

The PQR isn't a separate ETL — it reuses the exact query layer that powers the dashboard, so every cell in the workbook is reachable from the UI.

Step 01
Source systems

LOS, LMS, Collections feeds land via /api/ingest/* endpoints with API-key auth, Zod validation, FX conversion.

Step 02
PQR tables

Pre-aggregated metrics: consumer_overall_metrics, consumer_product_metrics, roll_rate_series, vintage_points, non_starters, collection_metrics.

Step 03
Query layer

src/lib/queries/consumer.ts · 757 lines of typed Supabase queries. Same code serves the dashboard and the PQR.

Step 04
Excel build

src/lib/excel/consumer-pqr.ts · runs 14 queries in parallel, builds 14 sheets, triggers a browser download.

Operating the report
12 / 14

Running the Consumer PQR.

Three steps. Honors the dashboard's scope selection so the report always matches the view in front of you.

  1. Pick your scope. Group, Region, or Subsidiary — the same selector that filters the dashboard determines the PQR contents.
  2. Open the Consumer Finance tab. The export action is contextual to the active tab — being on Consumer routes you to generateConsumerPQR().
  3. Trigger the export. Fourteen queries run in parallel, the workbook is built in-browser, and a tab-coloured .xlsx downloads with a scope-stamped filename.

SubsidiarySingle-entity detail in local currency & USDSubsidiary CRO, local risk & product team
