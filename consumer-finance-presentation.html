01 The need
02  /  15

Why a PQR dashboard at all.

A monthly workbook traded by email is fragile. Risk decisions wait on whoever opens the right tab. The dashboard surfaces the same data live, scope-aware, and queryable by language.

The Excel-only era

A 30-tab workbook compiled once a month and circulated as an attachment. Reconciling across 5 subsidiaries meant copy-paste, manual FX, broken formulas.

In the dashboard

Live data, auto FX conversion to USD, validated by Zod schemas at ingest, and exportable back to Excel with one click for offline use.

Then

One file, one scope. Looking at a single subsidiary required a fresh workbook, often days later.

Now

Group, Region, and Subsidiary toggle in place. Every metric, every chart updates against the chosen scope.

Then

"What's our 90+ DPD trend on unsecured by subsidiary?" meant ten minutes of tab-flipping.

Now

Ask the AI panel in natural language. Gemini reads live portfolio context and answers in markdown with the same numbers.

Then

Threshold breaches were spotted only by readers familiar with the benchmark conventions.

Now

The risk appetite registry flags RAG status against configurable thresholds at Group, Region, Subsidiary, and product levels.

03 Conventions
04  /  15

How every sheet is structured.

Six conventions used throughout the workbook. The same shape reappears in every tab, which is why a reader who learns one moves quickly through the rest.

i

Period columns

Month-end periods sit across the top (Apr'25, May'25, Jun'25, Jul'25, Aug'25). Reading left to right traces the trend.

ii

Metric Type then Metric

Rows are grouped first by category (Book Size, Entry Rates, Portfolio Performance, Roll Rates, Process Efficiency), then by individual metric.

iii

Benchmarks inline

A Bench-mark column appears next to the latest period. Reading "10-12%" or "< 5%" beside the value tells you immediately whether the metric is in tolerance.

iv

Total then cuts

Most sheets carry a Total block first, then repeat the same structure for Secured, Unsecured, and key products.

v

Nine DPD buckets

Current, 1-30, 31-60, 61-90, 91-120, 121-150, 151-180, 180-210, 210+, plus FWOF (forward write-off). Consistent across Net Flow, Collection, Roll Rate sheets.

vi

Vintage cohort tables

Cohort rows by origination month, MOB columns (1 through 7). X+ and 30+ rates side by side.

15 Quick reference
15  /  15

Abbreviations and file paths.

The terms that recur across every sheet, with the source files for code review.

AUM
Assets Under Management. Total outstanding loan balance.
DPD
Days Past Due. Number of days an account is delinquent.
MOB
Months On Book. Age of the loan since origination.
FPD
First Payment Default. Loan that missed the first payment.
NCL
Net Credit Loss. Write-offs minus recoveries, over AUM.
ENR
Exposure at Risk. Outstanding balance on flagged facilities.
FWOF
Forward Write-Off. Amount expected to be written off.
ROI
Return on Investment. Interest yield, weighted by AUM.
LA
Loan Amount. Original disbursed amount of the loan.
NTC
New To Credit. Customer with no prior bureau record.
POF
Portfolio Outflow. Percent of balance leaving the book.
TDD
Through The Door. Application and bureau metrics at entry.
