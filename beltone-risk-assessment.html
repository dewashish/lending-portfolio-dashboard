Assessment Purpose

Assessment of Beltone (Egypt) risk readiness across the subsidiary stack. Objectives: validate maturity across all risk types, identify control gaps, evaluate alignment with Avalora Group risk frameworks, and define a prioritised remediation plan.

ii

Top findings.

01

Scalability & Data Integrity High

The current technology landscape is not conducive to growth. Over-reliance on manual processes poses a direct risk to data accuracy and operational resilience. Factoring & Leasing is the only organised stack. A clear, actionable technology upgrade plan is required.
02

Fraud Risk High

Identified incidents require immediate attention. Lack of automation and weak controls increase exposure. Root-cause analysis, corrective action plan, and formal incident documentation must be prioritised without delay.
03

PQR Standardisation High

PQRs are submitted in multiple formats across entities, fragmenting group-level monitoring. Group prototype PQR dashboard has been presented to Beltone Risk & IT. Execution plan with IT required; follow-up call to be scheduled on forward-looking scenarios.
04

Performance Risk (Risk-team HR) High

Risk function lacks defined objectives, structured KPIs, formal documentation, and clearly articulated roles & responsibilities. Needs a formal HR governance framework with measurable risk outcomes.
05

Operational Risk & RCSA High

RCSA process has been kicked off but risk-mitigation and control plans are not yet in place. Segregation of duties shows material weaknesses. Maker-checker absent in core teams.
06

Governance & Compliance High

Non-compliance with policies; risk control over subsidiaries is weak. Lack of standardised reporting and trend visibility limits oversight. Group frameworks shared; alignment review pending.

Company-level observations.

Microfinance

Pronounced key-man dependency, with decision-making heavily centralised on a single individual. Limited appetite for technology adoption; preference for manual processes. Treat as a Group Risk priority.

Seven

Lack of advanced technology infrastructure, absence of maker-checker controls, and continuation of outdated practices are collectively limiting the company's growth potential. Modernisation roadmap required.

Factoring & Leasing

The only entity with reasonably organised processes. Use as a reference baseline for the rest of the Beltone stack; codify operating model and apply to peers.

Recommended next steps.

  1. Stand up a 90-day Beltone risk programme under Group Risk oversight, anchored on the priority actions above.
  2. Embed a Group Risk officer at Beltone for the first quarter to drive RCSA closure and SoD design.
  3. Lock the PQR rollout with Beltone IT and schedule the forward-looking scenarios working session.
  4. Mandate tech roadmaps for Microfinance and Seven, with monthly progress reviews.
  5. Convene a Beltone Board risk session to communicate findings and secure ownership at the highest level.
Out of scope Cyber risk was not reviewed in this assessment. It sits within the CTO's purview. Relevant observations will be routed to Saket's team for resolution and tracked separately under the group cyber forum.
